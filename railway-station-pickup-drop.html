Convenient Train Station Transfers

Whether you are arriving in Mangalore by train or travelling to catch a departure, you can contact Nayara Taxi Service to arrange local transportation. Share the station name, train schedule and final destination when requesting a booking.

Who Can Request a Transfer?

  • Visitors arriving for holidays
  • Families travelling with luggage
  • Business travellers and professionals
  • Residents travelling to or from railway stations
  • Passengers continuing to nearby towns, subject to route availability

Help Us Plan Your Pickup

Send the station name, train number if available, expected arrival or departure time, pickup point, destination and passenger count. Please allow time for train delays and confirm the meeting point with the driver before travel.
