Travel and Return with One Plan

A round trip taxi is a practical option when you need transportation to a destination and back to Mangalore. Tell us your itinerary, travel dates and expected return time so we can check the suitable vehicle and quote for your journey.

Whether your trip is for a meeting, family occasion, sightseeing or a personal visit, the booking can be discussed around your schedule and route.

Popular Round Trip Uses

  • Business meetings and appointments
  • Family functions and celebrations
  • Temple visits and pilgrimages
  • Outstation sightseeing and holidays
  • Visits to nearby cities and towns

What to Share When Booking

  1. Pickup address in Mangalore or nearby areas.
  2. Destination and any planned stops.
  3. Departure date, return date and approximate timings.
  4. Passenger count and preferred vehicle.
  5. Ask whether waiting, tolls, parking and driver allowance are included in the quote.
